Upon the ice, where southern oceans freeze,
The penguins gather, dressed in black and white,
They waddle forth beneath the pale starlight,
And brave the bitter Antarctic breeze.

Through howling winds and subzero degrees,
They huddle close to last the polar night,
A colony of warmth, a steadfast sight,
United strength against the endless freeze.

They dive beneath the waves with graceful art,
Through currents cold, they chase the silver fish,
Then surface, calling out to find their mate.

No force of storm can tear their bonds apart,
For faithfulness is every penguin's wish—
A love as vast as ice, as sure as fate.
